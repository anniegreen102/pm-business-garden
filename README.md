# The PM Business Garden

An interactive, sketchbook-style canvas for product managers to map the business side of their product, across five pillars:

1. **Customer**: ICP, B2B/B2C/B2B2C, user vs. buyer persona, SMB vs. enterprise, horizontal vs. vertical
2. **Landscape**: competitors, alternatives, disruptors, differentiation
3. **Monetization**: business model, pricing, unit economics (LTV, CAC, payback)
4. **Retention**: usage frequency, retention curves, churn, activation, product-market fit
5. **Growth**: positioning, go-to-market, funnels vs. growth loops, upsell & cross-sell, network effects

…plus PM principles to finish. Earn seeds, grow from Seed to Ancient Oak, collect badges, and get a one-page Business Brief with "weeds to pull" (blind spots) you can copy as Markdown.


## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Answers are saved only in your own browser's local storage and are never sent to any server, so nobody else can see what you enter. Use "Replant from scratch" in the sidebar to clear them.

The page uses [PostHog](https://posthog.com) for anonymous usage stats: page views, a few milestones (a section finished, the brief exported, the garden reset) and session replays of how the page is used. Your answers are masked in replays and never included in any event.
